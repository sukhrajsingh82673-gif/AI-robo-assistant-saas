# ZONATA V4 — ENTERPRISE PILOT READINESS & ROLLOUT PLAYBOOK
**Customer Onboarding, Data Ingestion Strategy, & Phased Rollout**

## 1. Pilot Rollout Framework
- **Phase 1: Foundation Setup (Days 1–3)**
  - Tenant provisioning, SSO federation (Okta / Entra ID), SCIM directory connection.
  - Role and department organizational hierarchy modeling.
- **Phase 2: Corporate Data Plane Hydration (Days 4–7)**
  - Bulk ingestion of non-sensitive knowledge base (`INTERNAL`, `PUBLIC`).
  - Pre-retrieval RAG benchmark validation and department ACL verification.
- **Phase 3: Governed AI Employee Deployment (Days 8–14)**
  - Deployment of 5–10 read-only analyst AI Employees.
  - Daily spend ceilings set to strict pilot caps ($50/day).
- **Phase 4: Mutating Actions & Expansion (Days 15–30)**
  - Gradual enablement of mutating tools with human-in-the-loop approvals.
  - Review of Merkle Evidence Ledger and Lineage DAG trails with enterprise auditors.
